Gate #1 · The intake · 121 questions · ~20 minutes

The intake form is the qualifier.

Everything in this funnel exists to drive one action: completing the intake. By design it's heavy: a parent who won't invest 20 minutes and 121 questions is not close to a $5–13k decision. Completion is the filter that separates the leads worth human time and a personalized close from the ones who exit to nurture. Nothing past this gate fires without it.

✓ Intake completed → The Plan

Holding message → tailored plan + kit + team + price → two-button close. The human layer opens (see the The Plan tab).

× Never completed → exit

No call, no price, no individual attention. Drop to monthly nurture; re-enter anytime if they complete it later.
