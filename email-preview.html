Four tags, all of them already collected. Every one comes from screen 1 of the intake and is already sent to the CRM as child_first_name, parent_first_name, parent_last_name and email. Nothing new has to be captured to send this.
Two things this email deliberately does not do. It names no clinician, because the copy is signed by the team rather than a person, and it quotes no figure from the summary. The prices, the weekly cost and the recommendation all live in the attachment, where the disclosures sit beside them. Lifting a number into the email would separate it from the qualifying language.
Internal reference. The email copy here is the source of truth for what goes into the CRM template. The attachment it describes is built from intake-results.html.
